Why Choose Us

The Pastime difference

Every store sells old games. Very few sell them with expertise, care, and prices that don't insult your intelligence. We do all three.

01
Fair & Transparent Pricing

No gouging. No nonsense. Chris does the research so you don't have to — and the result is pricing that regulars keep coming back for, especially on Pokémon cards and retro titles.

02
Everything Works. Guaranteed.

Every cartridge, every console, every disc — tested before it hits the shelf. Customers report zero returns and total confidence in every purchase.

03
A Community, Not Just a Store

Regulars don't just shop here — they belong here. Chris knows his customers, their interests, and how to make every visit feel like hanging out with a friend who happens to know everything about retro gaming.

04
Clean Space, Wide Selection

Room to breathe, room to browse. Our Camarillo location is organized, accessible, and stocked with a diverse range that grows every month.
